Harat

Durable long-term memory for AI agents—so you never have to explain the same context twice.

Stop re-teaching your tools every morning.

Working with AI agents today feels like onboarding a new teammate every few hours. The moment a conversation compacts, a session expires, or you switch to another tool, hard-won decisions and rules vanish—and the agent makes the same mistakes all over again.

Harat gives agents persistent memory that stays intact across different tools, separate conversations, context compactions, and cache resets. What matters surfaces automatically at the right moment without bloating prompts or breaking continuity.

Currently in closed beta—built so engineering teams and product builders can turn stateless AI sessions into reliable, long-running collaborators.
