AX

A working thesis on agent-mediated interaction—and why the chat box is not the endgame for software interfaces.

Beyond the chat box.

As software becomes increasingly agent-driven, generating and rendering static UI is rapidly ceasing to be the bottleneck. Yet most AI products still funnel complex, multi-step intent through a single linear text box.

AX (Agentic Experience) is our ongoing working thesis and empirical exploration into post-chat interaction paradigms. When the majority of human-machine interaction flows through an autonomous agent rather than manual clicks, the core design challenge shifts from layout to orchestration, delegation, feedback, and trust.

We believe a foundational standard for building agentic experiences is emerging—much like GUI conventions once standardized desktop computing. AX is our effort to define, prototype, and test those primitives in the open.
