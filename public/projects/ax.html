Working Thesis · Active Research

AX

An ongoing working thesis on why the chat box isn’t the endgame—and how software changes when agents mediate the experience.

The interface is no longer the bottleneck. The experience is.

For decades, software meant clicking through fixed menus and screens. Now agents are taking over the work—yet almost every AI product still forces rich, multi-step goals through a single text box.

AX (Agentic Experience) is our ongoing research into what comes after chat. As generating interfaces becomes trivial, the real challenge shifts to the experience around delegation: how people express intent, stay in control, verify outcomes, and let reliable systems handle routine execution while AI focuses strictly on decisions.

Just as early personal computing converged on shared interface standards, we believe a new standard for building agent-driven experiences is emerging. AX is our open effort to test those ideas in real workflows and help shape what comes next.
