Egwe

An autonomous drone swarm built to operate without the internet—engineered for real Nigerian terrain and weather.

Open Live Simulation → Discuss Egwe

Autonomy that works when connectivity doesn’t.

Most autonomous drone systems assume clear skies, flat terrain, and constant cloud connectivity. Across much of Nigeria, real operations happen under dense foliage, heavy rain, Harmattan haze, and zero cellular coverage—where cloud-dependent hardware simply stops working.

Egwe solves this by letting a fleet of low-power drones coordinate locally as a single hive mind—grounded in Nigerian atmospheric and climate research—without needing the internet at runtime. Because the decision logic is distilled down to lightweight math, it runs smoothly on minimal onboard hardware instead of expensive, power-hungry compute.

The result is accessible, resilient aerial coordination that agricultural, logistics, mapping, and security teams can adapt and deploy in the real world. You can test the swarm behavior live in our interactive browser simulation at egwe.dexx.systems.
