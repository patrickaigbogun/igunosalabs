Egwe

A hive-mind drone swarm model trained on simulated multi-condition flight data, grounded in Nigerian atmospheric and climate research.

Swarm coordination built for real environments.

Most autonomous flight models are trained and validated under clean, temperate assumptions. In practice, real deployment across West Africa means coping with Harmattan dust, shifting wind shear, heat, and variable visibility.

Egwe is a coordinated hive-mind swarm model trained across simulated environmental conditions derived from Nigerian academic atmospheric research. Instead of treating each aircraft in isolation, the swarm coordinates state, formation, and resilience collectively.

As with all Igunosalabs R&D, Egwe is being developed in the open so engineering teams, researchers, and commercial operators can adapt and deploy the findings in real-world missions.
