Unose

Voice AI that actually understands and speaks Nigerian Pidgin and Nigerian English—naturally and at edge speed.

People shouldn’t have to fake an accent to use AI.

Global voice systems routinely miss Nigerian cadence, everyday expressions, and natural code-switching between English and Pidgin. Hundreds of millions of speakers are left with voice assistants, customer support tools, and hardware interfaces that constantly misunderstand them.

Unose is a complete speech family built directly on Nigerian Pidgin and English corpora: Unose-Huen for accurate transcription (reaching 7.71% error rate and outperforming stock Whisper on natural Nigerian cadence), Unose-Voice for lightweight, natural speech synthesis, and Unose-Uwa for end-to-end spoken understanding.

Designed to run fast on ordinary hardware and released openly so startups, telecoms, and device makers can ship voice products that feel native from day one.
