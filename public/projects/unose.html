Unose

A family of speech models trained on Nigerian Pidgin and English corpora to understand and speak like Nigerians—with state-of-the-art performance.

Voice AI that actually speaks the language.

Mainstream speech recognition and synthesis models routinely break down on Nigerian English, Nigerian Pidgin, and natural code-switching—forcing hundreds of millions of speakers to adapt their voice to machines instead of the reverse.

The Unose family of models is trained directly on curated Nigerian Pidgin and English speech corpora to both understand and generate natural, authentic speech, with flagship checkpoints reaching state-of-the-art accuracy.

Built as open research that product teams, voice platforms, and hardware makers can deploy commercially.
