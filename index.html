Mission

Bridging intelligence and physical reality

Digital intelligence has scaled rapidly, but operating reliably in unstructured physical environments remains one of the hardest open problems in engineering. We focus on foundational R&D that moves AI out of screens and into the real world.

Focus Areas

Core Research Pillars

Our work spans hardware-aware learning, sensorimotor control, and reproducible physical benchmarks.

Embodied Control

Policies and control architectures that enable robots to manipulate, navigate, and adapt under real-world physical constraints.

Physical Perception

Multimodal sensing—vision, tactile, and proprioceptive fusion—built for noisy, dynamic environments.

Sim-to-Real Transfer

Closing the reality gap with physically grounded simulation, system identification, and low-cost hardware validation.

Open Commercialization

Non-profit research. Commercial-ready.

As a non-profit, Igunosalabs exists to accelerate the entire field. Our papers, datasets, models, and reference designs can be adopted, modified, and deployed by startups, enterprises, and independent builders for commercial products.
