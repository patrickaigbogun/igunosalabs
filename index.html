03 // Open Commercialization

We do the research. You build the industry.

Igunosalabs is structured as a non-profit so our breakthroughs become public industrial infrastructure—not proprietary choke points.

When we publish a model, control stack, benchmark, or reference hardware blueprint, commercial entities are explicitly encouraged to take it into production. No restrictive academic-only clauses. No hidden royalty traps.

By removing the R&D barrier at the base of the stack, we accelerate every company deploying intelligent machines into manufacturing, logistics, energy, and critical infrastructure.
